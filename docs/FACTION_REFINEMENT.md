# Hell & Back RTS — Faction Refinement

## Purpose

This document refines the faction presentation for the website and marketing.

These are not just lore descriptions. They are conversion tools.

## Locked Factions

Do not rename:

- Vanguard
- Ascendancy
- Hive

## Vanguard

### Title
Frontline Dominance

### Description
Hardened boots on broken ground. Rifle drills, drop pods, the smell of cordite.
Vanguard wins by showing up first — and refusing to leave.

### Identity
- Stable
- Reliable
- Pressure-based

### Tags
- Versatile
- Forgiving
- Mid-Range

## Ascendancy

### Title
High-Tech Specialists

### Description
Fewer hands on the trigger — every one of them carries a small star.
Lattice shields. Phase blades. Weapons that rewrite the next ten seconds of the fight.

### Identity
- Precision
- Control
- High skill ceiling

### Tags
- Elite
- Tech-Locked
- High Skill

## Hive

### Title
Swarm Supremacy

### Description
A wave you hear before you see. Hive trades bodies for tempo.
By the time you react, they are already inside your base.

### Identity
- Aggressive
- Fast
- Overwhelming

### Tags
- Swarm
- Aggressive
- Glass Cannon

## Website Rule

Each faction must:

- Be readable in under 2 seconds
- Have a clear playstyle
- Feel different from the others
- Be visually color-coded

## Conversion Hook

Always include a decision moment:

"Which faction would you command?"

This drives engagement and increases waitlist conversions.
